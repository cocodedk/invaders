# World 10: Hollow Night (plan, 2026-10-25)

A tenth world. The user asked for "another level" in a vampire and Halloween theme, and said the
harbour's detail was what they meant by its aesthetics. They accepted the pitch: an original vampire
lord (no character names), Neon Rain's `darkness` twist, and a second
breather a step easier than world 9, about worlds 3–4, with worlds 1–9 untouched.

## Feature-decide pass

- **Question.** The need is more game, themed for Halloween (six days away), with the harbour's level of
  detail. The user named the theme, the difficulty and the twist.
- **Deleted.**
  - No new formations, bullet patterns, powers, HUD or music engine.
  - No new twist: the night reuses Neon Rain's `darkness` (twists.js), which needs no code. It
    dims the scene, the world's own lights keep burning, and lightning lights it all every 6–10 s.
  - No new boss mechanic: the Count's bat swarm is drawing plus the existing diver minions.
  - The curve of worlds 1–9 stays as it is, and the boss does not `charge`.
- **Simplified.**
  - World 10 sets its own `d` in the existing `OWN` override (difficulty.js).
  - Art reuses the parallax, ambient, flourish, sky-body, bunker and pickup registries wherever one
    fits. Ronin Moon's `petals` work as falling leaves, and the existing `embers` and `mist` stay.
- **Jev** (order-averaged, 4 orders):
  - Bat swarm: `art_plus_divers` (p 0.97, confidence 0.91).
  - Cards: `three_cards`, as for world 9 (p 0.91, confidence 0.92).

## Content

- **World** 10 "Hollow Night": a haunted hill on Halloween night under a huge harvest moon. The twist
  is `darkness`. The boss kind is `count`, named "Crimson Count".
- **Difficulty** `d` for w10-1..5 starts at 0.30, 0.32, 0.34, 0.36, 0.38 (w3-1 is 0.26, w4-1 0.38).
  Tune it until the headless clear rates sit between world 3's and world 4's. `darkness` is visual
  only, so the autopilot cannot feel it, which is a reason to err easy.
- **The darkness is the show.** When the night dims, what keeps burning must be the picture:
  - the carved faces of the jack-o'-lanterns;
  - the ghosts' eye holes;
  - the cauldron's green brew;
  - the castle's windows;
  - the Count's red eyes and crimson heart.
  The aliens' and boss's light passes (`drawAlienLights` and `drawBossLights`) and the layer
  `lights` must carry these. Lightning should throw the castle and the dead trees into stark relief.
- **Boss**, the Crimson Count. A towering vampire lord, pale-faced and red-eyed, with a high collar and
  a black cape spread wide like bat wings over a blood-red lining. A glowing crimson heart-amulet sits
  on his chest, and his clawed hands are at the cape's lower corners.
  - **Rest and size:** about y 250 and 700 × 340, bottom edge no lower than the behemoth's.
  - **Parts:**
    - collar (armor, top);
    - head (armor; the eyes shoot);
    - heart (weak, centre bottom);
    - wingL/R (armor, the cape, wide outside);
    - clawL/R (weak, the hands, low).
  - **Phases:**
    - sway → hover (the cape opens) → orbit (the swarm: the drawing breaks him into a cloud of bats
      around the heart).
    - Attacks: aimed blood bolts from the eyes, fans from the claws, a ring and a spiral from the
      heart, homing bats from the wings.
    - Bat divers come from the wings, and a carrier comes every phase, every 4.5 s (R55).
    - About as hard as the shogun or a little easier; not as hard as the behemoth.
- **Look.** The colours are ours, not other worlds': pumpkin orange and witch-fire green on violet
  black, not Neon Rain's cyan and pink, nor the harbour's teal.
  - **Sky:** violet-black to bruised purple, with an amber glow at the horizon.
  - **Moon** (sky body `harvestmoon`): a huge moon, bone-white with faint craters, an orange halo
    and thin cloud bands across it.
  - **Far layer:** a gothic castle on a crag, with spires, battlements and warm lit windows.
  - **Mid layer:** twisted dead trees and a crooked chapel belfry.
  - **Near layer:** a graveyard of iron fences and tilted headstones, with lit jack-o'-lanterns on the
    ground.
  - **Ambient:** orange and brown leaves tumbling (`petals`), green will-o'-wisp motes (`embers`),
    and low ground fog (`mist`).
  - **Flourishes:** a bat flock that crosses the moon now and then, a rare witch on a broom
    silhouetted against the moon, and the darkness's lightning.
- **Aliens**, the night's guests:
  - grunt: a sheet ghost with a wavy hem and glowing eye holes;
  - diver: a bat with ragged wings and red eyes;
  - gunner: a jack-o'-lantern with a carved, glowing face that spits embers;
  - armored: an iron-banded coffin whose lid cracks open as it is hurt;
  - carrier: a bubbling witch's cauldron.
- **Bunkers:** headstones (skin `gravestone`): weathered stone, moss and cracks, no lettering.
- **Pickup frame** `FRAMES[10]`: a pumpkin rind with carved notches.
- **Music:**
  - A new mood, `gothic`: an organ pad, harpsichord-like plucked arpeggios, a tolling low bell on
    the bar and soft timpani.
  - Scale: a new `harmonic` (harmonic minor, `[0, 2, 3, 5, 7, 8, 11]`), root D, bpm about 112.
  - For the boss: a bell toll with a bat shriek (`ROAR`) and an organ lead (`LEAD`).
- **Trailer:**
  - A violet film tint, `#a35cff`, and gothic title type.
  - Original lines only. The tagline is "Midnight keeps its guests."
  - A tenth lobby poster.

## Outcome

- **Difficulty.** The first draft was too hard where it had gunner rows, `fan`/`rain` patterns and
  8-wide waves: w10-4 cleared 7/20 at lag 10 and 3/20 at lag 15, and w10-2 cleared 5/20 at lag 15.
  Easing those two levels brought w10-1..5 to 19/17/18/17/19 at lag 10 and 17/15/15/12/20 at lag 15:
  about world 4, and easier than world 9.
- **Darkness.** At Neon Rain's 66 %, the detail the user asked for only showed in the lightning. So a
  look can set `dark` (screen-view.js), and world 10's is 0.38: moonlit. Neon Rain is unchanged.
- **Boss.**
  - The bat swarm keeps circling the heart's place after the heart falls, which is usually before
    the last phase.
  - The Count rests at y 270, not 250, so his collar and face stay clear of the HUD at the top of his
    orbit (as R28 did for the shogun). He clears 19/20 at lag 10 and 20/20 at lag 15.
- **Engine.** `music.js` gained two drum letters, `b` (bell) and `p` (soft timpani), for the gothic
  loop. The other worlds' patterns don't use them.
