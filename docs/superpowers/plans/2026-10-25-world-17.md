# World 17: Ash to Air (plan, 2026-10-25)

The user asked: "create another world where cigarettes and tobacco and addictions must be defeated and
health is achieved and make the diffculty 10/10 yet send help once in a while". The look, the rules
and every detail beyond that are mine.

## The idea

A city choked by smoke. Colossal cigarettes stand among the towers like factory chimneys, ash falls
like grey snow and the sun is a dull coin behind the smog. The enemies are the habit itself:
cigarettes, cigars, pipes, smoke and the packs they come in, led by the Craving, a giant of smoke on a
throne of ash. The fight is the hardest in the game, but help comes: every so often a line of small
friendly craft sweeps up the screen on a wave of fresh air, and every enemy bullet the wave touches is
gone. And the city heals as you win: level by level the smog thins, the sky turns from brown to blue,
and trees, parks and kites come back. When the Craving falls the air is clear.

## Feature-decide pass

- **Question.** The user asked for five things: tobacco and addiction as the enemy, health achieved,
  difficulty 10/10, help now and then, and (as always) a world of my own.
- **Deleted.**
  - A world-only power. Nobody asked for one this time (Jev: false, 0.15).
  - Ally ships that join for a while, and a health pickup dropped every so often (the other two
    help candidates). Jev leaned to the fresh-air wave (0.55, under the gate, the other vote for the
    allies), so I settled it: the wave. It is the most visible rescue under heavy fire, it needs no
    catching, and drawing small friendly craft at its front makes it read as help *sent*.
  - Brand names, logos, packaging art and people smoking. The packs are plain, the billboards say
    nothing a brand would, and nothing in the world shows smoking as appealing.
  - Help that heals lives, shields the ship or hurts aliens. The wave only wipes bullets.
- **Simplified.**
  - The help is world 17's twist, `breath`, a rule on a timer like the saucer's or the glitch's.
  - The healing sky is drawing only, keyed to the level and to how far the level has gone (Jev:
    true, 0.95). No rule changes with it.
  - `OWN[17]` in difficulty.js. Commits 034947a (world 16) and f7a807a (world 15) as templates.
- **Settled.** Difficulty about world 7's, the hardest in the game but no harder (Jev: 0.94). The user
  called world 7's titan "a crazy level", winnable but at the limit. Pushing follows the user's
  standing "merge when ready and cleanup".

## Rules (core)

- **Twist** `breath` (world 17 only, new).
  - A timer in the twist state: the first breath comes `HELP_FIRST` = 14 s into the level, then one
    every `HELP_EVERY` = 24 s, waves and boss alike. Both are tuning knobs.
  - A breath is a front that rises from the bottom of the field (y 1080) to the HUD's edge (y 96) in
    `BREATH_TIME` = 0.8 s. Every enemy bullet the front passes over is wiped (`life = 0`, and
    `near = true` so a wipe never scores a near miss, R37). Beams (the laser pattern) are not
    wiped. Use a swept test on the front so nothing slips past it in a tick.
  - Events: `breath { y0, y1, dur }` as it starts, and `cleanse { x, y }` for each bullet wiped.
  - Aliens, the boss, the ship, pickups and the ship's shots are untouched.
  - The front's height is a function of the twist state, so the drawing and the rule agree (export a
    `breathFront(world)` that returns y, or null when no breath is rising).
- **Difficulty:** `d` for w17-1..5 starts at 0.86, 0.89, 0.92, 0.96, 1.0. `armorBonus` applies.
  Tune with the help included to world 7's clear rates (40 seeds, lag 10 / 15, on 034947a):
  30/29/18/15/6 and 20/4/7/3/2. The boss should end near the titan's (6 and 2), never harder.

## Content

- **World** 17, "Ash to Air".
- **Boss:** kind `craving`, "The Craving": a towering giant of smoke enthroned in a colossal ashtray,
  ember eyes, a burning maw that inhales, arms of smoke ending in grasping claws, and a crown of
  half-smoked cigarettes.
  - **Rest and size:** about y 260 and 740 × 340, with nothing reaching into the HUD (no art above
    y -158 from its centre, the crown included).
  - **Parts:**
    - `crown` (armor, top: the crown of cigarettes);
    - `maw` (weak, centre: the burning mouth);
    - `clawL`/`clawR` (armor, wide outside: the smoke arms' claws);
    - `eyeL`/`eyeR` (weak, at the sides of the head: the ember eyes);
    - `tray` (weak, centre bottom: the ashtray's glowing rim, where its butts smoulder).
  - **Phases:** sway → hover (it rears, the maw drawing breath, smoke thickening) → orbit (it
    thrashes, embers raining: drawing, plus diver minions).
  - **Attacks:** aimed from the eyes, fans from the claws, rain (falling embers and ash) from the
    crown, ring from the tray, laser in the last phase from the maw. It is a world-7-hard boss.
  - **Minions:** divers from the claws, and a carrier every 4.5 s (R55).
  - It never charges.
- **The healing** (drawing only). A heal amount `h` from 0 to 1 that the art computes from the world:
  - w17-1..4: `h` runs from (level − 1) × 0.2 at the level's start to (level − 1) × 0.2 + 0.2 at
    its last wave, by waves cleared;
  - w17-5: from 0.8 at the start to 1 as the boss loses its health, and 1 once it is dead.
  - `h` is eased per frame so it never jumps.
  - At `h` 0 the sky is brown-grey smog with a dull sun, ash falls thickly, and the city is grey with
    the giant cigarettes smoking. As `h` rises the smog thins and lifts, the sky turns blue, the sun
    brightens, the ash thins to nothing, the giant cigarettes go out and crumble, and green comes
    back: trees, park lawns, kites and birds. At 1 it is a clean, bright morning.
- **Look** (at `h` 0, healing as above).
  - **Sky:** a low smog ceiling, sickly amber-brown, the sun a dull coin; at 1, clear blue.
  - **Far:** a skyline of towers with colossal cigarettes standing among them like chimneys, their
    smoke merging into the smog.
  - **Mid:** older blocks, rooftop water towers, dead trees, empty billboards (no brands, no words a
    brand would use); greening as `h` rises.
  - **Near:** a street of ash-grey kerbs, gutters full of butts, a park fence; flowers and grass come
    back with `h`.
  - **Ambient:** falling ash and drifting smoke wisps, thinning with `h`; at high `h`, pollen and
    birds.
  - **Flourishes:** smoke rolling across the skyline, a cigarette chimney's ember pulsing; at high `h`,
    kites rising, a flock of birds, sunbeams breaking through.
- **The breath** (the twist's art and sound): the front is a bright band of fresh blue-white air with
  leaves and sparkles, led by a line of small friendly craft (white and green, a leaf emblem: help on
  its way). Each wiped bullet bursts into a tiny sparkle. As the breath passes, the smog it crosses
  parts for a moment. Sound: a deep fresh inhale swelling into a bright major chord as it starts; tiny
  bright ticks for the wipes, pitched by x and throttled.
- **Aliens:**
  - grunt: a lit cigarette, its ember tip glowing and a curl of smoke rising;
  - diver: a flicked cigarette butt that dives trailing smoke;
  - gunner: a tobacco pipe that puffs as it fires;
  - armored: a fat cigar in a plain band, its ash flaking and cracking as it is hurt;
  - carrier: a plain cigarette pack, no brand. Destroying it frees what it was holding: its pickup.
- **Bunkers:** skin `filter`, clean-air filter blocks: white, green-trimmed panels that grey with
  soot as they are carved.
- **Pickup frame** `FRAMES[17]`: a ring of green leaves.
- **Music:** a new mood: driving and urgent, a heartbeat kick and a rhythm of breath (filtered noise
  inhale and exhale), pulsing bass, and a soaring hopeful lead. No smoky jazz or blues, which would
  flatter the habit. Its root, scale and tempo must differ from every other mood. For the boss: a
  monstrous wheezing inhale and a rasping roar (`ROAR`), and a bright, clean lead (`LEAD`).
- **Trailer:** a film tint distinct from the other sixteen, smog amber clearing to blue (pick one
  tint). Tagline "Take back every breath."
- **Lobby:** a ninth card in "Also showing". The side row is now the main row's grid of eight, so a
  ninth needs a new fit. Every world stays reachable and readable, there is no page scroll at
  1280/1600/1920, and reels stay legible with six-digit scores.

## Outcome

- **The help counts for the slower hand.** On the final Craving (120 seeds) the breath is worth
  nothing at lag 10 (11 clears with it and without) and half again at lag 15 (9 against 6). On an
  early draft it looked worth little (25/12 with it, 22/12 without, 40 seeds). A wipe every 24 s
  clears the field for a moment; a quick bot dies to what comes next anyway. It stays as the user
  asked for it; the user chose to try the fight as it is.
- **`rate` is the boss's pace, not its fire.** Raising the Craving's rates from 0.9 to 1.8 changed
  nothing. What made it world-7-hard was what makes the titan so: homing shots and a laser in the
  hover phase, more hp (268), and a low rest (380, the lowest boss), so its shots start near the
  ship. On 120 seeds it falls 11 and 9 times against the titan's 12 and 6, and dies the same way:
  most runs end in the hover phase.
- **40 seeds is too few for a boss this hard.** A 4-hp trim moved it from 8/2 to 13/6, and faster
  claw fans once made it *easier* at lag 10. The final call was made on 120 seeds.
- **Level 1 needed a fourth wave.** Three waves cleared 37/35; a 6-wide gunner-and-grunt fan wave
  brought it to 26/19 (world 7: 30/20). An 8-wide one overshot to 20/12.
- **The Craving's hp is bounded by the bot rule** (40–60 s for the stand-and-fire bot): 272 took
  60.3 s, so the maw went to 92.
- **Lobby:** nine side cards at the main row's own column width, centred, half a column off the
  posters; up and down go by `alike(rows)` in `lobby-nav.js`.
- **The music was replaced (2026-10-26).** The user found the driving loop "very very annoying" (the
  racing heart, the hissing breaths and the supersaw lead). It is now a clear morning: E flat major at
  104 bpm, a struck FM electric piano over the glass world's choir, the archive's plucked bass, a soft
  kick and a hat, with no noise swells. The sound effects are unchanged (their degrees sit the same in
  the major), and the loop is trimmed to -26.4 dB like the others.
