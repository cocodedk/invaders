# World 12: Starlit Archive (plan, 2026-10-25)

The user asked me to "create a new world with over average difficulty all by yourself. don't ask me
anything. it is the world of your fantasies." So every choice here is mine.

## The idea

A library at the edge of the universe, where every star is a book. Shelves rise like cathedral
towers into an indigo nebula, golden constellation lines draw themselves between the stars like
gilding on a manuscript, pages drift on the air, and the guards of the stacks are made of paper,
ink and bronze. A world made of words is the one I'd dream of.

## Feature-decide pass

- **Question.** The user wants more game: a world that is mine, harder than average. The only
  constraints they set are the difficulty and that I decide alone.
- **Deleted.**
  - No new formations, bullet patterns, powers, HUD, music engine or core rule.
  - No new twist and no new boss mechanic.
  - No change to worlds 1–11 or the main curve.
  - No boss charge: worlds 9–11 never charge, and the user found a low boss cramped (the titan).
- **Simplified.**
  - `OWN[12]` in difficulty.js.
  - The look, parallax, ambient, flourish, sky-body, bunker and pickup registries.
  - Commit 48e8503 (world 11) is the template for every file a world touches.
- **Settled by me, not Jev.** The user asked for my own choices, so the calls below are mine. None
  of them is irreversible or outward-facing; the push is after the user's earlier "merge when
  ready".

## Content

- **World** 12, "Starlit Archive": the library at the edge of the universe.
  - **Twist:** Code Rain's `glitch`. Aliens blink between formation slots, and a near miss drops
    into 0.5× bullet time (8 s cooldown). It is above-average in difficulty, like world 6. It gets
    its own look and sound ([[themed-twist-effects]] in the vault):
    - **Blink:** an alien dissolves into a whirl of pages and gold dust, and re-forms in the new slot.
    - **Frame:** the glitch bands become a gold-ink smear, a page turning across the frame.
    - **Bullet time:** "the ink holds its breath": a warm sepia-gold tint.
    - **Sound:** a blink is a page flutter plus a harp note in the world's scale, pitched by where it
      lands. Bullet time is a held string chord in the scale, swelling in and out.
    - Code Rain looks and sounds exactly as before, with a test pinning it.
  - **Boss:** kind `grimoire`, the "Grand Grimoire".
- **Difficulty:** `d` for w12-1..5 is 0.64, 0.66, 0.69, 0.71, 0.74, world 6's own run (25/39 =
  0.641 to 29/39 = 0.744). `armorBonus` applies, as in world 6. Tune until the headless clear rates
  sit near world 6's (probe w6 first), the boss near the agent core's.
- **Boss, the Grand Grimoire.** A colossal living spellbook, open, floating upright:
  - A bronze-bound cover with gilt corners.
  - Pages that fan and flutter like wings, script crawling across them in gold.
  - A great sigil-eye opening on the open spread.
  - Two quills that write its attacks in the air.
  - A jewelled clasp hanging from the spine.
  - **Rest and size:** about y 260 and 700 × 340, with the face clear of the HUD.
  - **Parts:**
    - `crown` (armor, top: the spine's gilded headband);
    - `eye` (weak, centre: the sigil-eye on the spread);
    - `pageL`/`pageR` (armor, the wide open pages);
    - `quillL`/`quillR` (weak, low at the page corners);
    - `clasp` (weak, centre bottom).
  - **Phases:** sway → hover (more pages fan out, and the script burns brighter) → orbit (the book
    tears loose a storm of pages circling it: drawing, plus diver minions).
  - **Attacks:** aimed from the eye; fans and spirals from the quills; ring from the clasp; homing
    from the pages; laser from the eye in the last phase.
  - **Minions:** divers (paper cranes) from the pages, and a carrier every 4.5 s (R55).
- **Look.** Indigo, warm gold and parchment, with a single teal accent. Not Hollow Night's violet,
  nor Aurora's green.
  - **Sky:** a deep indigo nebula with a spiral galaxy, dense stars, and golden constellation lines.
  - **Far:** shelf towers rising like spires, with arches, spiral stairs and lit windows, and a
    great domed reading hall with a rose window.
  - **Mid:** floating islands of stacked books, with reading lamps, ladders, rope bridges and a
    brass orrery turning.
  - **Near:** a marble gallery balustrade, with candles, open books whose pages turn, and ink pools
    holding the stars.
  - **Ambient:** drifting pages, gold motes, and candle embers.
  - **Flourishes:** constellations that draw themselves star to star, then fade; a flock of paper
    cranes crossing; a comet; a gust of loose pages.
- **Aliens:**
  - grunt: an ink wisp, a blot with eyes that trails ink;
  - diver: a paper crane;
  - gunner: a floating spellbook that snaps open to fire;
  - armored: a bronze owl bookend, which cracks as it is hurt;
  - carrier: a lantern moth with glowing wings.
- **Bunkers:** stacks of old tomes (skin `tomes`): leather spines, gilt bands, worn corners.
- **Pickup frame** `FRAMES[12]`: a gilded border of gold leaf.
- **Music:**
  - A new mood, `archive`: harp arpeggios, music-box bells, a string pad, a pizzicato bass and a
    soft frame drum.
  - Scale `dorian`, root D, bpm about 116.
  - For the boss: a thunderous book slam with a rush of pages (`ROAR`), and a solo violin lead
    (`LEAD`).
- **Trailer:**
  - A warm gold film tint, distinct from the other eleven.
  - Elegant serif title type.
  - Original lines only. The tagline is "Every star is a story."
- **Lobby:** the fourth card in the "Also showing" row. Check it fits.
