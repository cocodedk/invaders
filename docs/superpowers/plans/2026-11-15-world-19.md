# World 19: Rubber Regatta (plan, 2026-11-15)

The user asked: "plan and build a new world which is funny to play too. ultracode. it must differ from
other worlds". The theme, the rule and every detail beyond that are mine, settled after a judged panel
of six concepts: the rule is the panel's Ruckus Pond (prototyped), the world is its Rubber Regatta.

## The idea

A sunlit bathhouse at toy scale: an invading fleet of bath toys sails in over mountains of foam. It is
the game's first comic world, bright and daylit. Nothing you shoot here dies. It is knocked silly,
hops with a squeak and tumbles down as a harmless, cross-eyed rubber toy, and if you fly the ship
into it you bat it away like a ball: it bowls through its friends, banks off the tiles and bonks the
boss on the head. Many bonks are happy accidents (a body falls onto the ship, a chain runs on by
itself) and they are half the comedy; the bat you aim is for the boss, where it pays (measured
below). The joke is always on the toys, because a knocked-out body can never hurt the ship.

## Feature-decide pass

- **Question.** Three things were asked: a new world, funny to play (the play itself, not only the
  look), and unlike the other eighteen.
- **Deleted** (each named by the panel or its judges).
  - A world-only power, the Bounce Mat included: it chains by itself, which is world 14 again.
  - The boss's gulp (she swallows a body and spits it back): cut for scope.
  - Regatta's bouncing toy physics: wall and HUD rebounds, hit lists, kick cooldown, two-mode flick,
    per-role kick factors, the one rng draw per knock-out. Pond's single whack rule stays.
  - A diver knocking itself out on a body, a boss attack cancelled by a bonk, bodies hitting other
    bodies, a fallen boss part as a body, the dotted aim line, a strike clip in the trailer's picker
    (a chain is already a multi-kill there), a chain score bonus (the combo already pays), a rebound
    counter on screen, a second backdrop reaction.
  - The `homing` pattern in this world's waves and boss: dragging bullets is world 18's verb.
  - Everything tin, clockwork or wind-up (world 14), teal (worlds 9, 11, 15), a rose window or an
    arcade of columns (world 16), sea bubbles (world 15).
  - A prize for a strike (a pickup drops), and a kick cooldown: the first bought nothing when measured
    (below), and the second is not needed: a rebounding body cannot be batted while it still rises,
    which is one condition on fields the body already has.
- **Simplified.**
  - One rule, `bonk`, in two small pure files, with one hook line in `hurtAlien`. It draws no rng and
    is gated on the world, so worlds 1-18 replay unchanged and `RUN_V` stays 11.
  - The motion is called **tumble**, never gravity, and bends no bullet (worlds 8 and 15 own wells).
  - The comedy on a kill is a re-skin of `kill` and `explode` in fx and sound, not a core flag.
  - The backdrop answers play in one way only (the taps spurt on a strike), by reading a tagged flash
    from fx; the core knows nothing of it.
- **Settled.** Mid-hard, near worlds 12 and 13 (`armorBonus` on: the stoppers need their hp). The user's
  standing "dont make it easy" holds. The rule is R71, the next free number in the spec.

## Rules (core)

**Twist `bonk`** (world 19 only, `level.twist === 'bonk'`). State: `world.twist.bodies`, a plain array
in knock-out order, made in `createTwist` for this twist alone. A body is
`{ id, role, x, y, vx, vy, r, fly, link, banks, bonks, daze, t }`.

1. **Knock-out.** An alien or boss minion that `hurtAlien` brings to 0 hp is scored, combos and drops
   as anywhere, and leaves a dizzy body where it fell. The body hops: `vy` starts at `hopFrom(y)` and
   rises by `TUMBLE_G` a second until it sinks at `SINK`; `vx` is 0. No rng. `hopFrom(y)` is `-HOP`,
   or 0 where the hop's peak (`HOP ** 2 / (2 * TUMBLE_G)`) would lift the body's centre above
   `HUD_CLEAR` (`formations.js`): no body hops behind the HUD or off the top. At `BODY_MAX` live
   bodies the alien simply dies. `BODY_MAX` is a safety bound that play does not reach (the
   autopilot's worst moment, a bomb on a full wave, made 49 bodies), so nothing you shoot goes without
   its body. A rammed alien and the minions wiped at the boss's death leave none (neither goes through
   `hurtAlien`).
2. **Whack.** A dizzy body the ship's path touches this tick (`pathDist2(ship, body) <= WHACK_R ** 2`,
   ship alive) is launched at `LAUNCH` px/s from where the ship's move began (`fromOf(ship)`) through
   the body's centre, or straight up when they coincide. It works while the ship blinks or is
   shielded and costs nothing. The body gets `fly = true`, `link = 0`, `banks = BANKS`. A body still
   rising on a rebound cannot be whacked: a rebound sets its `daze` to `DAZE` (`HOP / TUMBLE_G` s, as long as the
   hop rises), counted down while it is dizzy, and it is batted only once that is over (within `EPS`). Under
   the HUD, where `hopFrom` gives the rebound no hop, the daze still holds (review, 2026-11-15: `vy < 0` alone
   let a parked ship land `BONK_MAX` there in four ticks), so
   a ship parked where it rebounds lands one bonk a bat, not `BONK_MAX` in five ticks.
3. **Bonk.** A flying step first saves `from`, the body's position before the move. The body is then
   tested along its path against the first live alien (`firstAlien`), else the first live boss part
   (`firstPart`, `liveParts` taken once a tick). `bonk` is emitted first, with `ko = a.hp <= BONK`,
   then the damage: `BONK` to an alien, `BONK_BOSS` to a part through its own rule (weak 100 %,
   armour 25 %).
   - **Knock-out:** the striker is spent, and the new body flies on at once with the striker's
     velocity and banks, at `link + 1` (the bowling chain). The cap counts bodies not yet spent, so a
     chain is never cut by it. When the new body's `link` reaches `STRIKE_AT`, `strike` is emitted,
     once a chain.
   - **Survivor or boss part:** the striker rebounds. It goes back to `from` (never `x - vx * dt`,
     which on a bank tick lies beyond the wall), dizzy again (`fly = false`, `vx = 0`,
     `vy = hopFrom(from.y)`, `link = 0`, `daze = DAZE`), and `bonks += 1`. At `BONK_MAX` it is spent instead
     (`fizzle`).
4. **Bank.** A flying body with a bank left reflects once when this tick's move crosses a wall from
   inside: `(x < lo && from.x >= lo) || (x > hi && from.x <= hi)`, with `lo = EDGE + ALIEN_R` and
   `hi = WIDTH - EDGE - ALIEN_R` (`bank`). A body knocked out beyond a wall (divers and minions reach
   x = `EDGE`, boss carriers enter from off screen) just flies on. The hit test runs on the bank tick
   too.
5. **Ends.** A body that leaves the field is gone; one that leaves by the bottom plops (`plop`).
6. **Never.** A body never hurts the ship, never blocks a shot, beam, bomb or bullet, never holds a
   wave open, and lives through the wave gap. Live aliens and boss parts still ram as in world 18.

Per tick (`applyTwist`, after `collide`, so the ship's path is final): loop `i < n` with `n` taken at
the start, so a body made this tick steps from the next, and a body already spent is skipped; then
prune. `state-hash.js` adds a body term only when bodies exist (count, then id, x, y, fly, link and
bonks of each), so every other hash is unchanged.

| Constant | Start | File | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| `HOP` | 260 px/s | `tumble.js` | a fresh or rebounding body's upward speed |
| `TUMBLE_G` | 520 px/s² | `tumble.js` | how fast the hop turns into a fall |
| `SINK` | 180 px/s | `tumble.js` | the fall's top speed |
| `BODY_MAX` | 60 | `tumble.js` | live bodies at once: a bound, not a rule of play |
| `BODY_R` | `ALIEN_R` | `tumble.js` | a body's radius |
| `WHACK_R` | `ALIEN_R + SHIP_R + 8` | `bonk.js` | the ship's reach |
| `LAUNCH` | 1000 px/s | `bonk.js` | a batted body's speed |
| `BONK` | 2 | `bonk.js` | damage to an alien |
| `BONK_BOSS` | 15 | `bonk.js` | damage to a boss part, before its rule (at 5 batting did not pay) |
| `BANKS` | 1 | `bonk.js` | wall bounces a launch gets |
| `BONK_MAX` | 3 | `bonk.js` | rebounds a body lands before it is spent |
| `STRIKE_AT` | 4 | `bonk.js` | knock-outs in one chain that make a strike |
| `DAZE` | `HOP / TUMBLE_G` | `bonk.js` | how long a rebounded body cannot be batted, hop or not |

**Events** (added to `test/event-sample.js`): `whack { id, role, x, y, dx, dy }`;
`bonk { id, role, x, y, link, ko }` (on a part: `role: 'boss'`, `part`, x and y the body's);
`bank { x, y }`; `strike { x, y, links }`; `plop { x, role }`; `fizzle { id, role, x, y }`. The usual
`kill`, `explode` and `hit` still fire.

**Difficulty.** `OWN[19] = [0.64, 0.67, 0.70, 0.73, 0.76]`, so `armorBonus` is on throughout (a Squirt
Frog has 3 hp, a Sponge 4: both survive one bonk and stop a chain). Card G tunes it.
