# World 14: Ember Carnival (plan, 2026-10-25)

The user asked: "create another world of your own with a new twist". The theme, the twist and every
detail are mine. World 13 brought a power and a twist because the user asked for both; this one asks
for a twist only.

## The idea

A night fair on the edge of a lake during its fireworks festival. A Ferris wheel turns over the
midway, big-top tents glow, string lights sway, and rockets climb from the far shore. The invaders
crashed the show, and every one of them is a toy stuffed with powder: shoot a lit one and it goes up
like a firework, setting off the ones around it.

## Feature-decide pass

- **Question.** The user asked for a new world and a new twist, of my own. Nothing else.
- **Deleted.**
  - A new power. Nobody asked for one this time (Jev: false, 0.00).
  - A chain score bonus. Chained kills score as ordinary kills (Jev: false, 0.23).
  - Every-kill bursts. On a grid of one-hit grunts, 120 × 84 px apart, one shot would clear a whole
    wave. Only marked shells burst (Jev: shells only, 0.99, margin 0.98).
  - Sparks as real shots. They would raise questions about bunkers, boss parts and what they stop
    on. The burst is area damage through `damage.js`, the one place that knows what can be hurt, and
    the sparks are only drawn.
  - The burst cancelling enemy bullets, hurting the ship, a fuse sound, a HUD counter, a new formation
    and new ship shots: nobody asked, and the burst's flash and rising pitch already show the chain.
- **Simplified.** The twist is one flag on an alien (`shell`), one queue of lit fuses, and one helper
  in `damage.js`. It is gated on `level.twist === 'fireworks'`, so worlds 1–13 replay unchanged and
  `RUN_V` stays.
- **Settled by me.** The theme, the numbers and the art. None of it is irreversible. Pushing follows
  the user's standing "merge when ready and cleanup".

## Rules (core)

- **Twist `fireworks`** (world 14 only).
  - **Shells.** Each alien created in world 14 (a wave's, or a boss minion) is a shell with chance
    `SHELL_SHARE` (start at 0.22), drawn from the world rng only in world 14. A shell has
    `shell: true`. Other aliens have no `shell` field at all.
  - **The fuse.** When a shell dies, however it dies (shot, beam, bomb, burst), its fuse is lit. After
    `FUSE` = 0.12 s it bursts where it died.
  - **The burst.** It deals 1 damage to every target within `BURST_R` = 160 px of its centre: aliens,
    and boss parts measured to their boxes. Boss parts take the damage through their usual armor rule.
    It never hurts the ship, bunkers or bullets.
  - **The chain.** A shell killed by a burst lights its own fuse, one link deeper (`depth` + 1), so a
    cluster of shells goes off pop, pop, pop.
  - **Event.** `burst { x, y, depth, kind }` is emitted as each one goes off. `depth` is 0 for the
    first, and `kind` is the dead shell's role.
- **Difficulty:** `d` for w14-1..5 is 0.70, 0.72, 0.75, 0.77, 0.80, a notch above world 13 to pay for
  the bursts. `armorBonus` applies. Tune to world 6's clear rates on the headless run, and the boss
  near the agent core's.
  - The reference is world 6 on 48e8503 (40 seeds, lag 10 / 15): 39/30/34/21/17 and 34/19/27/14/4.
  - Tune in this order: `SHELL_SHARE`, then wave speeds and roles.

## Content

- **World** 14, "Ember Carnival".
- **Boss:** kind `carousel`, "The Grand Carousel": a flying carousel, all gilt and mirrors, its
  canopy striped, carved horses on brass poles, a band organ at its heart.
  - **Rest and size:** about y 260 and 700 × 340, with nothing poking into the HUD.
  - **Parts:**
    - `crown` (armor, top: the canopy's crown and pennant);
    - `organ` (weak, centre: the band organ's pipes and drum);
    - `horseL`/`horseR` (armor, wide outside: the carved horses);
    - `lampL`/`lampR` (weak, low at the sides: the hanging lanterns);
    - `drum` (weak, centre bottom: the turning drum under the floor, ringed with bulbs).
  - **Phases:** sway → hover (the canopy spinning faster, its bulbs chasing) → orbit (the horses
    galloping round it: drawing, plus diver minions).
  - **Attacks:** aimed from the organ, fans from the lamps, rain (confetti shells) from the crown, ring
    from the drum, laser in the last phase from the organ.
  - **Minions:** divers from the horses, and a carrier every 4.5 s (R55). Minions can be shells, so a
    lit one that bursts near the carousel hurts it.
  - It never charges.
- **Look.** Deep indigo night lit magenta, gold and teal by fireworks; smoke drifting across the stars.
  - **Sky:** indigo deepening to near black at the top, smoke haze, a slim moon behind the smoke, and
    firework bursts high up.
  - **Far:** the far shore's hills with a roller coaster's lit track and a car running along it;
    rockets climbing from the shore; the lake reflecting the bursts.
  - **Mid:** a Ferris wheel turning, its spokes lit and chasing; big-top tents with pennants; a
    helter-skelter tower.
  - **Near:** the midway: string lights swaying between poles, game booths with marquee bulbs, and
    balloons tied to them.
  - **Ambient:** drifting embers, confetti and a few sky lanterns rising.
  - **Flourishes:** firework shows (chrysanthemum, willow, ring) with falling sparks, a rocket rising
    on its trail, the Ferris wheel's lights chasing, and a searchlight sweep.
- **Aliens** (clockwork toys from the prize booths):
  - grunt: a tin wind-up drone, its key turning;
  - diver: a firework rocket with fins and a sparking tail;
  - gunner: a jack-in-the-box turret whose spring head pops up as it fires;
  - armored: a tin drummer automaton, which dents and sheds paint as it is hurt;
  - carrier: a bunch of prize balloons carrying a gift box with a lantern.
- **Shells.** A shell is drawn with a lit fuse fizzing on top and a faint glow through its body, on
  any role, so it reads at a glance.
- **The burst** ([[themed-twist-effects]]):
  - its own sprite: a chrysanthemum of sparks in the shell's colour, with trails, a white flash and
    a puff of smoke;
  - its own fx: sparks that fall and fade (within the 600-particle cap);
  - its own sound: a crackling pop on the world's scale, pitched by x, one scale step higher for each
    link of the chain.
- **Bunkers:** skin `booth`, striped red-and-cream boards with a row of bulbs on top.
- **Pickup frame** `FRAMES[14]`: a ring of marquee bulbs.
- **Music:**
  - A new mood, `carnival`: a calliope lead, oom-pah bass, glockenspiel and a snare-and-cymbal
    march.
  - A waltz if the sequencer can do triple time cheaply, otherwise a fast polka. Pick the root and
    scale so it differs from `armada` (G major, 132).
  - For the boss: an organ chord swelling over a ratchet crank (`ROAR`), and a steam-whistle lead
    (`LEAD`).
- **Trailer:** a film tint distinct from the other thirteen, magenta-violet. Tagline "One spark is all
  it takes."
- **Lobby:** a sixth card in "Also showing", with no horizontal scroll at 1280/1600/1920 and reels
  still legible with six-digit scores.
