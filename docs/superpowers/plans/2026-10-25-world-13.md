# World 13: Sunset Armada (plan, 2026-10-25)

The user asked: "create another world of your own, with some twists, one thing i imagine is a
superpower where the player gets sidekick/s for a minute or how long the other superpowers last and a
twist which you can devise, like firing side ways to left and right too for a while". The theme, the
twist and the details are mine. The two ideas are new rules, not reskins, so this world touches the
core more than worlds 9–12 did.

## The idea

A battle at golden hour over a sea of clouds. Brass-and-canvas airships sail between floating islands
whose waterfalls pour into the clouds. The sun hangs huge and low, cumulus towers burn gold, and
distant broadsides flash inside the clouds. It is a world built around the two new rules. The ship
gets escorts (the sidekicks), and fires broadsides like a ship of the line (the sideways fire).

## Feature-decide pass

- **Question.** The user named two features: a sidekick power that lasts as long as the other powers,
  and a twist of my devising, with sideways fire as the example. Everything else is mine.
- **Deleted.**
  - Wingmen everywhere: adding a kind to `POWERS` reshuffles every world's drops, changes balance
    the user tuned, and breaks old replays. Wingmen drop in world 13 only. Worlds 1–12 stay exactly
    as they are, and `RUN_V` stays.
  - Escorts that can be shot down, aim on their own, or fire anything but the ship's shot. They
    mirror the ship's fire, and that is the whole feature.
  - A HUD element for the broadside: the ship's side gun ports glow and dim over its time instead.
  - New formations: `dive` already swoops in from the sides, so world 13's levels lean on divers,
    and the side guns have something to hit.
- **Simplified.**
  - Wingmen are an ordinary power (10 s, the power ring, the reserve; R53), on a world-13 drop list.
  - The broadside rides the pickup event: any pickup caught in world 13 opens the side guns for 6 s.
  - `OWN[13]` in difficulty.js; the registries for everything drawn; commits 1ddc0b8 (world 12)
    and 48e8503 (world 11) as templates.
- **Settled by me.** The user asked for my own choices. None of them is irreversible, and pushing
  follows the user's standing "merge when ready and cleanup".

## Rules (core)

- **Wingmen** (power `wingmen`, world 13 only).
  - Two escort fighters fly beside the ship, ±80 px, and follow it.
  - Each escort fires a copy of the ship's straight shot whenever the ship fires, for the power's
    10 s.
  - They stack with nothing, like every power. A second power waits in the reserve (R53).
  - Drops in world 13 pick from `[...POWERS, 'wingmen']`. Every other world keeps `POWERS` and its
    rng draws unchanged.
- **Twist `broadside`** (world 13).
  - Catching any pickup (power, bomb or life) opens the ship's side guns for 6 s; another catch
    resets the time to 6 s.
  - While open, every volley also fires one shot straight left and one straight right, level with
    the ship.
  - Side shots die at the screen's edge, like any shot that leaves the field.
  - They hit what they meet: divers swooping at the ship's height, carriers and minions low down.
- **Difficulty:** `d` for w13-1..5 is 0.66, 0.68, 0.71, 0.73, 0.76: a notch above world 12, since
  the two rules help the player. `armorBonus` applies. Tune to world 6's clear rates on the headless
  run, the boss near the agent core's.
  - The reference is on main 48e8503 (40 seeds, lag 10 / 15): 39/30/34/21/17 and 34/19/27/14/4.

## Content

- **World** 13, "Sunset Armada".
- **Boss:** kind `galleon`, the "Thunder Galleon": a dreadnought airship, all brass hull, patched
  sails, propeller pods and gun decks.
  - **Rest and size:** about y 260 and 700 × 340, with nothing poking into the HUD.
  - **Parts:**
    - `mast` (armor, top: the mainmast and crow's nest);
    - `bridge` (weak, centre: the lit bridge windows);
    - `propL`/`propR` (armor, wide outside: the propeller pods);
    - `gunL`/`gunR` (weak, low at the sides: the gun decks);
    - `keel` (weak, centre bottom: the furnace glow).
  - **Phases:** sway → hover (sails reefed, the furnace roaring) → orbit (smoke and cannon fire
    rolling round it: drawing, plus diver minions).
  - **Attacks:** aimed from the bridge, fans from the gun decks, rain (dropped shells) from the mast,
    ring from the keel, laser in the last phase from the bridge.
  - **Minions:** divers from the props, and a carrier every 4.5 s (R55).
  - It never charges.
- **Look.** Sunset gold and rose over violet-blue shadows, cloud-white highlights. No world so far has
  a sky of clouds.
  - **Sky:** rose-gold at the horizon rising to violet-blue, a huge low sun with god rays, and towering
    cumulus lit gold.
  - **Far:** floating islands with waterfalls pouring into the clouds, a watchtower, and a far fleet
    of airship silhouettes.
  - **Mid:** airships drifting, with sails, rigging and turning propellers, and floating rocks with
    windmills.
  - **Near:** a sea of rolling cloud tops lit from below, with mooring balloons.
  - **Ambient:** cloud wisps, sun motes, and a few gulls.
  - **Flourishes:** god rays, a flock of birds crossing, an airship crossing far off, and cannon
    flashes inside the clouds.
- **Aliens:**
  - grunt: a brass rotor drone;
  - diver: a gull-winged glider;
  - gunner: a balloon gun-pod;
  - armored: an ironclad gunboat, which dents and smokes as it is hurt;
  - carrier: a cargo zeppelin with a lantern.
- **The ship in world 13.**
  - Wingmen are two small brass-and-canvas fighters on the ship's flanks.
  - The broadside shows as glowing gun ports on the ship's sides, a muzzle flash and a smoke puff at
    each side shot, and cannon-ball shots.
- **Bunkers:** stacked cargo crates and sandbags in netting (skin `cargo`).
- **Pickup frame** `FRAMES[13]`: a ship's-wheel ring. The wingmen pickup gets its own icon.
- **Music:**
  - A new mood, `armada`: a brass fanfare lead, a snare march, a string ostinato and timpani.
  - Scale `major` (added), root G, bpm about 132.
  - For the boss: a cannon salvo with creaking timbers (`ROAR`), and a horn lead (`LEAD`).
  - The new rules get their own sounds ([[themed-twist-effects]]):
    - a broadside shot is a cannon thump, pitched by side;
    - the escorts' shots are a light rattle;
    - opening the side guns rings a ship's bell, on the scale.
- **Trailer:** a warm rose-gold film tint, distinct from the other twelve. Tagline "The wind is on
  our side."
- **Lobby:** a fifth card in "Also showing". Four cards fill the row, so the side cards must get
  narrower.
