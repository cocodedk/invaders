# World 9: Tidal Siege (plan, 2026-10-24)

A ninth world, asked for by the user ("shall we build another stage?"). They picked a kaiju-harbour homage
and a "breather side world": about world 4–5 difficulty, with worlds 1–8 untouched.

## Feature-decide pass

- **Question.** The need is more game after beating the titan; world 9 is the vehicle the user chose.
- **Deleted.** No new formations, bullet patterns, powers, music engine or HUD. No new twist: the sea
  storm reuses Twin Suns' `gusts` (ruling R57, below). The difficulty curve of worlds 1–8 is not
  rescaled. It is not harder than world 8, and the boss does not `charge` (the titan's "big and low"
  complaint).
- **Simplified.** World 9 sets its own `d` through an override in `difficulty()`, so `/39` stays put.
  Art reuses the existing parallax, ambient, flourish, sky-body and bunker registries wherever one fits.
- **Jev.** build → `ask_first` (p 0.99); difficulty → `breather` (p 0.98, confidence 0.72, so the user
  confirmed it).

## Content

- **World** 9 "Tidal Siege": a night harbour in a storm, a sea beast rising out of the water. Twist:
  `gusts` (storm wind). Boss kind `behemoth`, name "Abyssal behemoth".
- **Difficulty** `d` for w9-1..5 = 0.40, 0.43, 0.45, 0.48, 0.50 (w4-3 is 0.436, w5-1 0.513).
- **Boss**, resting at y 255, 640 × 330 (bottom edge at y 420, leaving room above the zone). Parts:
  crest (armor, top), skull (armor), maw (weak, centre bottom, the breath), gillL/R (weak, sides),
  clawL/R (armor, low outside). Three phases: sway → hover → orbit. Attacks: aimed, fan, rain, laser
  (the breath), ring. Divers from the claws. One carrier per phase every 4.5 s (R55).
- **Look:**
  - Storm-night sky with a teal glow on the horizon.
  - Far city skyline, harbour cranes, dark sea waves with foam.
  - Sea spray and light rain.
  - Sodium-orange dock lamps against the beast's atomic cyan; no neon (that is Neon Rain's).
- **Aliens**, the beast's brood: grunt a crab, diver a manta ray, gunner an anglerfish with a lure,
  armored a shelled turtle-crab, carrier a glowing jellyfish.
- **Music:** a new mood `monster` with low war drums, brass-like stabs, a minor or phrygian scale and
  bpm about 96. A deep roar for the boss.
- **Trailer:** a cold teal film tint, monster-movie title type, and original lines only (no film titles
  or quotes). Tagline "The tide brings it home."
