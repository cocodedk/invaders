# World 11: Aurora Kingdom (plan, 2026-10-25)

The user asked to "plan another magnificent looking level". From four pitches they picked Aurora Kingdom
(a polar night under the aurora) and a difficulty of about world 5. That is a step harder than the two
breathers, so the side worlds span easy to medium. Worlds 1–10 stay untouched.

## Feature-decide pass

- **Question.** The need is more game, and above all a world that looks magnificent. The user picked
  the theme and the difficulty.
- **Deleted.**
  - No new formations, bullet patterns, powers, HUD or music engine.
  - No new twist, and no new boss mechanic.
  - No change to the main curve.
  - No lobby work: the "Also showing" row (branch `refactor/world-10-simplify`) takes any side world.
- **Simplified.**
  - `OWN[11]` in difficulty.js.
  - The parallax, ambient, flourish, sky-body, bunker and pickup registries.
  - Commits e546451 (world 9) and 92622e8 (world 10) as templates for every file a world touches.
- **Jev** (order-averaged): both answers came back below the gate, so I decided them.
  - **Twist:** `shuriken_shards` (p 0.76, confidence 0.67). The pitch said gusts, but gusts already
    blow in worlds 3 and 9. Ronin Moon's slow, large shots that the ship can cut become ice shards,
    which shatter, and that is more to look at.
  - **Boss charge:** unsure (p 0.44). It never charges, like worlds 9 and 10, because the user found
    the titan "big and low". The difficulty comes from its attacks.

## Content

- **World** 11, "Aurora Kingdom": a kingdom of ice under the polar night, the aurora burning over it.
  - **Twist:** `shuriken` (Ronin Moon's): enemy shots come as slow, large ice shards, and a ship's shot
    shatters one. It is drawn as an ice shard with a glassy shatter and chime, not steel.
  - **Boss:** kind `wyrm`, named "Crystal Wyrm".
- **Difficulty:** `d` for w11-1..5 starts at 0.51, 0.53, 0.56, 0.58, 0.60. w5-1 is 0.513 and w5-5 is
  0.615, and staying ≤ 0.6 keeps `armorBonus` off. Tune until the headless clear rates sit near world
  5's (probe w5 first), with the boss a little easier than the hive queen and harder than the behemoth.
- **The aurora is the show.** Two or three curtains of light ripple slowly across the top half of the
  sky:
  - a green core, with violet and magenta at the hems;
  - fine vertical rays that shimmer;
  - folds that travel along each curtain;
  - a shape that drifts over minutes, so it never looks looped.
  It is built from cached strips drawn as warped slices with `'lighter'`, within the Pi budget, and its
  light is echoed on the ice: pale green rims on the cliffs, and reflections shimmering on the water.
- **Boss, the Crystal Wyrm.** A serpentine ice dragon rising out of a glacier, with coils of
  translucent blue ice, a crest of crystal spikes, glowing frost-white eyes, and frost breath curling
  from its jaws.
  - **Rest and size:** about y 260 and 700 × 340, with the face clear of the HUD at the top of its
    orbit.
  - **Parts:**
    - crest (armor, top);
    - head (armor; the eyes shoot);
    - maw (weak; the frost breath);
    - gemL/R (weak, glowing crystal nodes in the coils);
    - coilL/R (armor, wide, low outside).
  - **Phases:**
    - sway → hover (the crest flares and the gems burn brighter) → orbit (a blizzard of crystal
      shards circles it: drawing plus diver minions).
    - Attacks: aimed bolts from the eyes, fans and `shuriken` shards from the gems, the frost-breath
      laser from the maw, an ice-nova ring, and hail (`rain`) from the crest.
    - Divers come from the coils, and a carrier comes every phase, every 4.5 s (R55).
- **Look.** The colours are ours: aurora green and violet on polar blue, with warm amber for the
  ship's accent. Not Event Horizon's pale blue, nor the harbour's teal.
  - **Sky:** polar blue-black to deep teal at the horizon, dense stars and a thin low crescent moon.
  - **Far layer:** glacier mountains with blue-shadowed faces and snow ridges, and an ice castle of
    spires catching the aurora, its windows lit pale gold.
  - **Mid layer:** ice cliffs with blue crevasses, and a frozen fleet: tall ships locked in the ice,
    tilted, with icicles on the rigging and a lantern still burning on one or two.
  - **Near layer:** ice floes on black water with the aurora shimmering in it, snowdrifts, and a frozen
    lighthouse whose beam sweeps through the haze.
  - **Ambient:** slow snowfall in layers, ice glitter catching the light, and diamond dust.
  - **Flourishes:** the aurora, shooting stars, the lighthouse beam, and a snow squall sweeping
    across now and then.
- **Aliens**, the kingdom's guard:
  - grunt: a frost spirit, a wisp with a cold glowing core and trailing frost;
  - diver: a snow owl with white wings and golden eyes;
  - gunner: a floating faceted ice crystal that fires the shards;
  - armored: an ice golem of packed ice and stone, which cracks blue as it is hurt;
  - carrier: a glowing narwhal whose horn is a light.
- **Bunkers:** glacier blocks (skin `glacier`): blue depths, trapped bubbles, a frost rim and a cap of
  snow.
- **Pickup frame** `FRAMES[11]`: a ring of frost crystals.
- **Music:**
  - A new mood, `aurora`: celesta-like bells (FM), choir pads (detuned voices, slow attack), a soft
    low drum and sleigh-bell shimmer.
  - Scale `lydian`, root E, bpm about 84.
  - For the boss: a glacier cracking and a wyrm's cry (`ROAR`) and a crystal-bell lead (`LEAD`).
  - The shard's shatter gets its own glassy sound in place of the steel shing.
- **Trailer:**
  - An icy film tint (about `#b8f4ff`, distinct from the other ten) and airy, thin title type.
  - Original lines only. The tagline is "The sky burns cold."
  - The poster is the wyrm against the aurora.
- **Lobby:** the third card in the "Also showing" row. Check it fits.

## Outcome

- **Balance.** World 5's reference (lag 10 / lag 15) is 20/8/13/10/7 and 14/7/9/3/2 out of 20 seeds,
  and at 100 seeds 99/62/69/48/35 % and 80/28/38/22/9 % (the queen last). World 11 as tuned, in %
  (40 seeds, the wyrm 60): 100/65/75/68/77 and 90/30/38/30/20. Its waves sit at about world 5's,
  and the wyrm between the behemoth (87/45) and the queen.
  - w11-1 first cleared 99 % at lag 15. More gunner rows and a fourth wave fixed it; faster
    waves alone barely moved it.
  - The wyrm is touchy in its sway phase: more hp made it easier (a longer sway is more drops), and
    10 % shorter intervals swung lag 10 from 85 % to 38 %. The middle step held.
- **Browser.** The aurora, the shards, the three phases and the lobby's third card were checked, and
  the console showed no errors. The crest's spikes poked into the boss bar at rest, so they were
  shortened by a quarter (art only).
- **Spec.** Ruling R59 and the world table row.
