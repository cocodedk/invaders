# World 16: Glass Cathedral (plan, 2026-10-25)

The user asked: "create another one please", after world 15. The theme, the twist, the power and every
detail are mine.

## The idea

A vast cathedral of stained glass, half-ruined and open to a dawn sky. Coloured light falls through
the high windows in slow shafts, and everything that lives here is made of glass and lead and stone:
beetles with rose-window shells, glass swallows, candle wisps in lanterns, gargoyles with stained-glass
wings. Shatter a gargoyle and its shards hang in the air; leave them, and they fly back together. One
power belongs to the cathedral alone: a mirror twin, a glass copy of the ship that flies opposite it
across the nave and fires when it fires.

## Rules (core)

- **Mirror** (power `mirror`, world 16 only).
  - While it runs, a glass twin flies at `{ x: WIDTH - ship.x, y: ship.y }`: the ship mirrored across
    the field's centre line. The zone is symmetric, so the twin is always inside it.
  - `twinOf(ship)` returns `[{ x, y }]`, or `[]` when the power is not running or the ship is gone, so
    the drawing and the rule agree. Same pattern as `escortsOf`.
  - Each trigger pull, the twin fires one straight shot from its muzzle, as an escort does, marked
    `mirror: true` for the drawing. The ship's own shot is the base shot.
  - The twin is untouchable: bullets, beams and aliens pass through it, and it collects nothing.
- **Drops.** World 16 picks from `[...POWERS, 'mirror']`. Every other world keeps its list and its rng
  draws unchanged.
- **Twist** `reassemble` (world 7's, unchanged): an armored death leaves a wreck for 2 s; shoot it or
  it stands up again. The levels lean on armored rows so it matters.
- **Difficulty:** `d` for w16-1..5 is 0.74, 0.77, 0.79, 0.82, 0.84, a notch above world 15.
  `armorBonus` applies. Tune to world 6's clear rates on the headless run, and the boss near the agent
  core's.
  - The reference is world 6 (40 seeds, lag 10 / 15): 39/30/34/21/17 and 34/19/27/14/4.

## Content

- **World** 16, "Glass Cathedral".
- **Boss:** kind `moth`, "The Vesper Moth": a colossal moth whose wings are stained-glass windows, lead
  cames dividing jewel-coloured panes, the dawn shining through them.
  - **Rest and size:** about y 260 and 760 × 340, with nothing reaching into the HUD. The antennae
    stay under y -158 from the boss's centre.
  - **Parts:**
    - `crown` (armor, top: the head and its feathered antennae);
    - `heart` (weak, centre: a rose-window jewel on the thorax);
    - `wingL`/`wingR` (armor, wide outside: the stained-glass forewings);
    - `spotL`/`spotR` (weak, low at the sides: the hindwings' eyespots);
    - `tail` (weak, centre bottom: the banded abdomen, glowing between its bands).
  - **Phases:** sway → hover (wings spread wide, the eyespots blazing) → orbit (wings beating faster,
    glass scales falling: drawing, plus diver minions).
  - **Attacks:** aimed from the eyespots, fans from the wings, rain (falling glass scales) from the
    wings, ring from the tail, laser in the last phase from the heart.
  - **Minions:** divers from the wings, and a carrier every 4.5 s (R55).
  - It never charges.
- **Look.** Dawn through coloured glass: rose, gold, cobalt and violet light on pale stone.
  - **Sky:** the cathedral's broken vault open to a dawn sky, rose low and violet high; slanting shafts
    of coloured light through high windows; dust in the light.
  - **Far:** the nave's tall pointed windows glowing in jewel colours, and one great rose window.
  - **Mid:** stone columns and flying buttresses, broken tracery, chandeliers swaying gently.
  - **Near:** shattered glass on the floor, iron candle stands, a broken carved arch.
  - **Ambient:** dust motes in the light, glints of drifting glass, candle sparks.
  - **Flourishes:** patches of coloured light sliding across the stone, glass doves crossing, a far
    bell swinging, the rose window's light slowly turning.
- **The mending** (the twist, re-skinned): the wreck is a hanging, slowly turning cloud of glass shards
  and stone chips. When it stands up, the shards fly together in a flash of coloured light. A wreck
  shot down scatters its shards glinting. Sounds on the world's scale, pitched by x: a glass shatter
  for the wreck, a rising reversed shimmer ending in a clink for the mending, a tinkle for a wreck shot
  down.
- **Aliens:**
  - grunt: a stained-glass beetle, its shell a tiny rose window;
  - diver: a glass swallow;
  - gunner: a candle wisp in a glass lantern, which flares as it fires;
  - armored: a stone gargoyle with stained-glass wings, which cracks as it is hurt;
  - carrier: a gilded reliquary on little glass wings.
- **The power's art and sound:**
  - The twin is a translucent glass copy of the ship with a prismatic rim, fading in and out with the
    power. Its shots are slim prismatic glass bolts, with a muzzle flash of their own.
  - Pickup icon in `ICONS[16]` (a ship and its glass reflection), which the HUD power ring draws too.
  - Sounds: a bowed-glass swell when it starts; a glassy ping layered under the ship's shot while it
    runs, as world 13's escorts rattle.
- **Bunkers:** skin `glass`, stained-glass panels in lead cames on stone sills.
- **Pickup frame** `FRAMES[16]`: a gothic quatrefoil of gold tracery set with glass jewels.
- **Music:** a new mood: a glass harmonica lead, choir pads, tubular bells and a soft frame drum. Its
  root, scale and tempo must differ from every other mood, and its sound from `organ swell` and
  `gothic`. For the boss: a great bell toll over a swelling organ cluster (`ROAR`), and bowed glass
  (`LEAD`).
- **Trailer:** a film tint distinct from the other fifteen, rose-violet. Tagline "Every color cuts."
- **Lobby:** an eighth card in "Also showing". The seven banners already fill a row as wide as the
  main row, so the side row needs a new fit. Every world stays reachable and readable, there is no page
  scroll at 1280/1600/1920, and reels stay legible with six-digit scores.
