# World 15: Lantern Deep (plan, 2026-10-25)

The user asked: "build one more round with your own fantasy and some special superpowers". The theme,
the powers and every detail are mine.

## The idea

The deep sea under a sunken kingdom. Light comes down from a surface nobody can see, kelp forests sway
over drowned spires, and everything that lives this deep makes its own light. A maelstrom turns
overhead and drinks the bullets. Two powers belong to the deep alone: a ring of pearls that guards the
ship, and a trident that runs through everything in its path.

## Feature-decide pass

- **Question.** The user asked for a world of my own and "some special superpowers". Nothing else.
- **Deleted.**
  - A new twist. Nobody asked for one this time (Jev: false, 0.12). World 15 reuses world 8's gravity
    well, re-skinned as a maelstrom with its own sprite, effects and sound ([[themed-twist-effects]]).
  - The powers in every world. That would reshuffle every world's drops, move tuned balance and break
    saved replays (Jev: false, 0.00). Like world 13's wingmen, they drop in world 15 alone.
  - A third power: a sea creature summoned to sweep the screen once. It is a bomb with a costume. Jev
    leaned to two (0.58, under the gate), so I settled it: two.
  - Pearls that can be shot down, aim on their own or block beams; tridents that cut through bunkers;
    any power stacking. Powers run one at a time with the reserve (R53), as now.
- **Simplified.**
  - Both powers are ordinary powers (10 s, the power ring, the reserve) on a world-15 drop list,
    `powersFor`, the way world 13's wingmen work.
  - The twist is world 8's `gravity`, unchanged in the core.
  - `OWN[15]` in difficulty.js. The registries for everything drawn. Commits 5a73adc (world 14) and
    cd58e7f (world 13) as templates.
- **Settled by me.** The theme, the numbers and the art. None of it is irreversible. Pushing follows
  the user's standing "merge when ready and cleanup".

## Rules (core)

- **Pearls** (power `pearls`, world 15 only).
  - Three pearls circle the ship, `PEARL_ORBIT` = 78 px out, a turn every 1.2 s, evenly spaced. Each
    pearl has radius `PEARL_R` = 13. Their angle comes from the power's time left, so they are exact.
  - A pearl wipes any enemy bullet it touches (`pearlBlock { x, y }`). It does not stop beams.
  - A pearl that touches an alien deals 1 damage (`pearlHit { x, y }`). That alien then takes no more
    pearl damage for 0.25 s.
  - The ship still fires its base shot.
  - `pearlsOf(ship)` returns their places ([] when the power is not running), so the drawing and the
    rule agree. This is the same pattern as `escortsOf`.
- **Trident** (power `trident`, world 15 only).
  - The ship's shot becomes a trident (r 7, 1 damage, the base rate). It passes through every alien and
    boss part in its path and hurts each once. The shot remembers what it hit.
  - It still stops at bunkers and still carves them.
- **Drops.** World 15 picks from `[...POWERS, 'pearls', 'trident']`. Every other world keeps `POWERS`
  and its rng draws unchanged.
- **Twist** `gravity` (world 8's well at the same place). It pulls every bullet, the ship's included,
  and swallows what reaches it (`absorb`).
- **Difficulty:** `d` for w15-1..5 is 0.72, 0.74, 0.77, 0.79, 0.82, a notch above world 14 because the
  two powers help. `armorBonus` applies. Tune to world 6's clear rates on the headless run, and the
  boss near the agent core's.
  - The reference is world 6 on 48e8503 (40 seeds, lag 10 / 15): 39/30/34/21/17 and 34/19/27/14/4.

## Content

- **World** 15, "Lantern Deep".
- **Boss:** kind `angler`, "The Lantern Leviathan": a colossal anglerfish, its lure blazing on a stalk
  over its head, its skin dark and dotted with lights.
  - **Rest and size:** about y 260 and 700 × 340, with nothing reaching into the HUD. The lure stays
    under y -158 from the boss's centre.
  - **Parts:**
    - `crest` (armor, top: the dorsal spines the lure's stalk rises from);
    - `maw` (weak, centre: the open mouth's glowing throat);
    - `finL`/`finR` (armor, wide outside: the pectoral fins);
    - `eyeL`/`eyeR` (weak, at the sides: the huge pale eyes);
    - `belly` (weak, centre bottom: the row of lights).
  - **Phases:** sway → hover (the jaw gaping, teeth lit) → orbit (its lights swirling, the lure
    flaring: drawing, plus diver minions).
  - **Attacks:** aimed from the maw, fans from the eyes, rain (falling spines) from the crest, ring
    from the belly, laser in the last phase from the maw.
  - **Minions:** divers from the fins, and a carrier every 4.5 s (R55).
  - It never charges.
- **Look.** Blue-black water, teal and violet bioluminescence, pale shafts of light from far above.
  - **Sky:** the water column, faint blue at the top where the light comes down in moving shafts,
    deepening to blue-black; marine snow; a far whale passing.
  - **Far:** a sunken kingdom's spires on a ridge, a few windows still lit; a giant whale's skeleton.
  - **Mid:** kelp forests swaying, coral arches, a sunken ship.
  - **Near:** the reef: anemones, glowing tube worms, sea grass, and a carved stone arch with lanterns.
  - **Ambient:** bubbles rising, marine snow, a few small fish.
  - **Flourishes:** a whale gliding far off, a school of fish wheeling, jellyfish drifting up, and
    caustic light rippling.
- **The maelstrom** (the twist, re-skinned): a slow whirlpool of bubbles and light in place of the
  well. Its own effect when it swallows a bullet: a bubble that spirals in and pops. Its own sound: a
  glassy bloop on the world's scale, pitched by x.
- **Aliens:**
  - grunt: a lantern jellyfish, its bell pulsing;
  - diver: a darting squid;
  - gunner: a pufferfish that puffs up as it fires;
  - armored: a hermit crab in an old diving helmet, which cracks as it is hurt;
  - carrier: a sea turtle carrying a lantern and a small chest.
- **The powers' art and sound:**
  - Pearls glow and leave short trails as they circle the ship. A blocked bullet flares the pearl,
    and a hit sparks the alien.
  - The trident shot is a glowing three-pronged bolt. It leaves a spark at each alien it passes
    through.
  - Pickup icons for both, in `ICONS[15]`, which the HUD power ring draws too.
  - Sounds: switching each power on (a glass chime for the pearls, a bright metallic ring for the
    trident); a soft glass tick for each block, pitched by x and throttled; and a watery zip for the
    trident shot.
- **Bunkers:** skin `reef`, coral-crusted stone blocks.
- **Pickup frame** `FRAMES[15]`: a ring of scallop shells and pearls.
- **Music:** a new mood, `abyss`: slow and deep, with a whale-song lead (sine glides), harp arpeggios,
  low pads and a heartbeat kick. It needs its own root, scale and tempo, distinct from every other
  mood. For the boss: a whale-like moan over a sub swell (`ROAR`), and a glassy theremin lead (`LEAD`).
- **Trailer:** a film tint distinct from the other fourteen, deep teal. Tagline "Never follow the
  light."
- **Lobby:** a seventh card in "Also showing". Six cards already fill the widest row the page allows,
  so the side row needs a new layout. Every world stays reachable and readable, there is no page
  scroll at 1280/1600/1920, and reels stay legible with six-digit scores.

## Outcome

- **Rules.** The pearls, the trident and the angler are world-15-only. The maelstrom is the gravity
  twist re-skinned.
  - `test/core/worlds-unchanged.test.js` now also pins w14-1, w14-3 and w14-5, taken on 5a73adc.
  - World 14 re-probed with identical counts (38/30/32/26/21 at lag 10).
- **Balance** (40 seeds): 39/31/31/21/23 at lag 10 and 32/15/27/11/8 at lag 15, against world 6's
  39/30/34/21/17 and 34/19/27/14/4.
  - The first draft was hard at lag 15 in levels 3–4. Both were thinned, and level 4 lost its laser
    wave, leaving four.
  - The maelstrom's well sits under the angler and swallowed the maw's aimed bolts, so the
    Leviathan was too easy. Its aimed fire moved to the eyes and the maw kept only the laser. That
    fits the fiction: the Leviathan lurks in the maelstrom's eye.
- **Lobby.** The seven side cards no longer fit as posters in one row. Each side card is now a
  10.2 × 3.5 rem banner over slimmer reels, so the row is 76.8 rem. No scroll at 1280/1600/1920.
- **Sound.** The abyss sub swell climbs 52 → 66 Hz, so world 9's sea monster keeps the lowest roar.
  `abyss` is the only mood in F♯ minor pentatonic at 54 bpm.
- **Boss art.** The first angler read as a mechanical bowl with goggles and a tiny lure, so a polish
  pass rebuilt it as a fish. It got a lumpy head, milky slit eyes, uneven fangs with an underbite,
  and a big cyan lure on a stalk arching over the crest. The lower jaw is a live sprite that drops
  open from the hover phase on, and the fins are translucent with a slow ripple. Part boxes are
  unchanged.
  - Left as is: when the boss dies, `drawDying` draws no live pass, so the stalk, lure and lower
    fangs vanish when the death chain starts, the same as every boss's live extras.
- **Review fixes.**
  - The trident's aqua streak now shows on a kill too. Before, it showed only on a hit, and a hit is
    never emitted for a 1 hp alien, so most bolts left no mark. A test covers it.
  - A poster bakes world 15's heavy sprites (caustics, maelstrom, sway) in an idle slice of its own,
    no longer inside the slice that draws its frame.
- **Spec.** Ruling R63 adds the world 15 entry, the counts, a table row, and a bullet for the
  world-only powers.
