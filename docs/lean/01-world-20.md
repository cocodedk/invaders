# World 20: Midnight Press

## Goal

Add a twentieth world, "Midnight Press", with a rule of its own (the echo), five levels, a boss and a
colophon that reads `built by graph skill fable-5.2 2026-11-18. cocode.dk`. The game goes from 19 worlds
and 95 levels to 20 worlds and 100 levels. Worlds 1-19 play exactly as before.

## Behaviour

### The world

- World 20, "Midnight Press": a great printing hall at night. Cream paper, ink black and one vermilion
  spot colour; sheets hang drying on lines like sails, presses stand as tall as towers, type drifts in the air.
- It is the twentieth entry of `WORLDS` (`src/core/levels.js`): id 20, twist `echo`, boss `editor`, free
  flight (`FREE_FROM` covers it), no bunkers. It appears in the lobby after world 19 the way every world
  does; no screen is added or changed.
- Five levels, `w20-1` to `w20-5`, in a new `src/core/levels-6.js`: four wave levels that follow the data
  rules `test/core/levels.test.js` enforces, and a boss level.
- Its own entry in `difficulty.js`, no easier than world 19's.
- Pars and ratings are regenerated by their scripts, never typed.

### The rule: the echo (twist `echo`, world 20 only)

- The ship has a carbon copy. It flies the ship's own path `ECHO_DELAY` seconds late: at every tick it
  stands where the ship stood `ECHO_DELAY` ago, and it fires a base shot on every tick the ship fired then.
- There is no copy for the first `ECHO_DELAY` seconds of a level.
- The copy's shots are ship shots: they hurt, kill and score exactly as the ship's do.
- Nothing hits the copy, and it collects nothing.
- While the ship is down, the copy flies on until the recorded path runs out, then waits for it.
- The state is plain data in `world.twist` (arrays only), no rng is drawn, and it is part of the state hash,
  so a replay of a world 20 run is exact. Nothing of it runs outside world 20.
- `ECHO_DELAY` and every other tuning value is a named, exported constant, defined once.

### The boss

- `editor`, the Night Editor: a colossal ink octopus in a green eyeshade with a red pencil in every arm.
  Like the other side-world bosses it never charges. Its phases and patterns are the builder's, in a new
  `src/core/bosses-data-7.js`, built from the existing bullet patterns.

### The look

- Design reference: world 19, Rubber Regatta, as it is in the game. World 20 has the same parts in the same
  places: a sky, three parallax layers, ambient particles, a cast of five aliens (grunt, diver, gunner,
  armored, carrier) as inky type creatures, and the boss.
- Its own ship, its own shot skin and its own power-up skins, in the hall's paper, ink and vermilion: the ship is a
  folded paper dart with an inked nib, and its shots are ink strokes. The carbon copy is the same ship drawn as a
  faint blue carbon tracing, and its shots are blue.
- Its own music mood, made the way world 19's is: a night-press mood (a slow mechanical pulse, like a press
  turning over) on a scale no other world uses.
- The colophon: a printed sheet in the near parallax layer, low on the right where world 19 has its brass
  plate (`src/ui/parallax-bath-plate.js` is the reference), reading exactly
  `built by graph skill fable-5.2 2026-11-18. cocode.dk`. It stays in the frame, clear of the HUD, and is
  readable at 1920x1080. The text is one named constant.
- The Pi limits hold: no per-frame `shadowBlur`, glow from cached sprites.

### The documents

- The design spec gets the rule as R72 and world 20's row in its Worlds table.
- `README.md`, `llms.txt` and `CLAUDE.md` say 20 worlds and 100 levels.

## Acceptance tests

`node:test`, in `test/`, reading the constants and never repeating their values:

1. `WORLDS` has 20 entries; the last is id 20, "Midnight Press", twist `echo`, boss `editor`, free. There are
   100 levels, and the level data rules hold for world 20.
2. In a world 20 level there is no copy before `ECHO_DELAY` has passed. After it, at every tick the copy
   stands where the ship stood `ECHO_DELAY` earlier, and a copy shot leaves on every tick the ship fired then.
3. A copy's shot that kills an alien scores what the ship's shot scores for it.
4. A bullet that crosses the copy flies on, and the ship's lives do not change.
5. A recorded world 20 run re-simulates to the same state hash, and two worlds that differ only in the
   copy's state hash differently.
6. In a level of any other world the echo state stays empty.
7. The fingerprints of worlds 1-19 are unchanged.
8. The colophon's constant is exactly `built by graph skill fable-5.2 2026-11-18. cocode.dk`.
9. The whole suite is green, and every code file is under 200 lines.

## Decisions

- The person, 2026-11-18, asked whether world 20 should reuse an existing ship, shots, power-up skins and music or
  get its own: "if possible innovate". So it gets its own, as The look says. `og.png` stays out.
- The grill asked whether the per-world rows the suite requires are in scope: the lobby tagline and poster time
  (`src/ui/screens/posters.js`), the trailer voice (`src/ui/trailer/cards.js`) and the trailer typography row
  (`TYPE` in `src/ui/trailer/draw.js`). They are in scope, and so is any other per-world row a test or the
  renderer requires for every world. The person said "use your imagination" (2026-11-18): the words and the
  typography are the builder's, in the voice of the other worlds.

## Out of scope

- A new kind of power-up (the skins are in scope, the kinds are the six standard ones).
- Any change to the trailer, the screens, the lobby's layout or the storage keys, beyond world 20's own per-world
  rows (see Decisions).
- Any change to how worlds 1-19 play.
- `og.png` and `og.html`, deployment, the Pi menu.
