# World 20, part 7.3: the world opens (the listing, pars and ratings, the lobby and trailer rows, the documents)

Part 3 of 3 of `docs/lean/017-world-20-opens.md` (World 20, "Midnight Press"). It builds on parts 7.1 and 7.2: the five levels (`src/core/levels-6.js`), the difficulty row, the registered boss art, the `TYPE` row and the film tint are in the repository. This part lists the world, which is what makes it playable. It is one card although it writes rows in `src/core` and two rows in `src/ui`: the suite compares these tables with each other (`WORLDS` with `TWISTS` and `BOSSES` in `test/core/levels.test.js:27-28`, `BOSSES` with `BOSS_DEFS` in `test/core/boss-data.test.js:14`, `PARS` with `LEVELS` in `test/core/pars.test.js`, `RATINGS`, `TAGLINES` and `VOICES` with `WORLDS.length` in `test/core/ratings.test.js:10`, `test/ui/posters.test.js:13` and `test/ui/trailer-cards.test.js:39`), so they can only go green together. Everything that could be listed ahead was, in parts 7.1 and 7.2. It draws nothing new.

## Goal

The game goes from 19 worlds and 95 levels to 20 worlds and 100 levels: World 20, "Midnight Press", is in `WORLDS` with five levels, its rule and its boss listed, its pars and ratings, its lobby, poster and trailer rows, and the documents say so. Worlds 1-19 play exactly as before.

## Behaviour

### The world

- It is the twentieth entry of `WORLDS` (`src/core/levels.js`): id 20, "Midnight Press", twist `echo`, boss `editor`, free flight (`FREE_FROM` covers it), no bunkers.
- `echo` joins `TWISTS` and `editor` joins `BOSSES` (`src/core/kinds.js`); the definition of part 2 (`src/core/bosses-data-7.js`) joins `BOSS_DEFS` (`src/core/bosses-data.js`).
- The five levels of part 7.1 (`src/core/levels-6.js`) join `LEVELS` as `w20-1` to `w20-5`: four wave levels and a boss level.
- It appears in the lobby after world 19 the way every world does; no screen is added or changed.
- Pars and ratings are regenerated by their scripts (`scripts/pars.mjs`, `scripts/ratings.mjs`), never typed.
- The suite's boss tests fight every entry of `BOSSES` on its level (`test/core/boss-kit.js`). Tuning the `editor` definition of part 2 so that they pass is in scope; its acceptance tests keep holding. World 20's own level data of part 7.1 may be adjusted for the same reason; its tests keep holding.
- Every tuning value is a named, exported constant, defined once.

### The per-world rows

- Decision (the grill and the person, 2026-11-18): the per-world rows the suite requires are in scope: the lobby tagline and poster time (`src/ui/screens/posters.js`) and the trailer voice (`src/ui/trailer/cards.js`), and so is any other per-world row a test or the renderer requires for every world and that parts 7.1 and 7.2 did not add. The person said "use your imagination": the words are the builder's, in the voice of the other worlds.
- Design reference: world 19's rows in those same files, and its card in the lobby as it is in the game.

### The documents

- The design spec (`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-17-invaders-design.md`) gets the rule as R72 and world 20's row in its Worlds table.
- `README.md`, `llms.txt` and `CLAUDE.md` say 20 worlds and 100 levels.

The pinned fingerprints of worlds 1-19 (`test/core/worlds-unchanged.test.js`, `worlds-idle.test.js`) do not move. If any other existing assertion fails, the builder stops and reports it; it is not changed.

## Open point for the person (found while splitting; the spec does not settle it)

`test/core/boss-editor.test.js:79-80` (part 2) asserts that the editor's signature is no entry of `BOSS_DEFS` and that `BOSS_DEFS` has no `editor` ("not listed yet"). Both fail the moment the definition joins `BOSS_DEFS`, which this part requires. The spec says the acceptance tests of part 2 keep holding and that any failing assertion it does not name stops the builder, so the two requirements cannot both be met as written. Part 6 met the same case and the decision there was that each such check skips world 20's own entry and still holds every other entry to the rule. Whether that decision applies here is the person's to say before this part is built.

## Acceptance tests

`node:test`, in `test/`, reading the constants and never repeating their values:

1. `WORLDS` has 20 entries; the last is id 20, "Midnight Press", twist `echo`, boss `editor`, free. There are 100 levels, and the level data rules hold for world 20 as listed.
2. The boss `editor` is fought to its end on `w20-5` and never charges.
3. The fingerprints of worlds 1-19 are unchanged.
4. World 20 has its lobby card after world 19 with its tagline and poster time, its trailer voice, and every other per-world row a test requires of every world.
5. The whole suite is green (`npm test`) with only the assertions named above moved, and every code file is under 200 lines.

## Out of scope

- Any change to the trailer, the screens, the lobby's layout or the storage keys, beyond world 20's own per-world rows (see the decision above).
- Any change to how worlds 1-19 play.
- Any new drawing or sound (parts 3-5).
- `og.png` and `og.html` (decision: `og.png` stays out), deployment, the Pi menu.
- A new kind of power-up.

## Decisions

2026-11-18, the open point above, answered by the runner on the person's standing word ("use your imagination", "stop for me if it is very necessary"), as part 6 decided the same case: in `test/core/boss-editor.test.js:79-80` the signature check skips the editor's own entry of `BOSS_DEFS` and still holds every other definition to the rule, and the "not listed yet" line becomes that `BOSS_DEFS` lists the `editor` exactly once. They are assertions that move, as far as said here and no further. Nothing else in that file changes.

2026-11-18, left open by part 1's reviewer, the builder's to settle ("use your imagination"): whether a laser beam counts as the ship having fired for the carbon copy. Any sensible choice works; the builder says which it took.

2026-11-18, found by part 7.1's reviewer, answered by the runner on the same standing word: two tests of part 7.1 in `test/core/levels-6.test.js` hold only while world 20 is not listed and fail once it is: "world 20 seeds are integers, distinct from each other and from every listed level" and "WORLDS and LEVELS are unchanged: world 20 is not listed yet". The seed test compares with the listed levels of worlds 1-19 only (it skips world 20's own), and the "not listed yet" test becomes that the listed levels of world 20 are the five specs of `levels-6.js`, in order. They are assertions that move, as far as said here and no further; the other tests of that file hold as they are.
