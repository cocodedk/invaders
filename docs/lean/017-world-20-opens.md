# World 20, part 7: the world opens (levels, registrations, per-world rows, documents)

Part 7 of 7 of `docs/lean/01-world-20.md` (World 20, "Midnight Press"). It builds on parts 1-6: the echo rule, the `editor` definition, the backdrop, the cast and boss art, the player's side, the music and the registered look are in the repository. This part adds the world itself to the data, which is what makes it playable. It is one card although it writes rows in `src/core`, `src/fx` and `src/ui`: the suite compares these tables with each other, so they can only go green together. It draws nothing new.

## Goal

The game goes from 19 worlds and 95 levels to 20 worlds and 100 levels: World 20, "Midnight Press", is in `WORLDS` with five levels, its rule and its boss listed, its difficulty, its pars and ratings, its lobby, poster and trailer rows, and the documents say so. Worlds 1-19 play exactly as before.

## Behaviour

### The world

- It is the twentieth entry of `WORLDS` (`src/core/levels.js`): id 20, "Midnight Press", twist `echo`, boss `editor`, free flight (`FREE_FROM` covers it), no bunkers.
- `echo` joins `TWISTS` and `editor` joins `BOSSES` (`src/core/kinds.js`); the definition of part 2 joins `BOSS_DEFS` (`src/core/bosses-data.js`) and the art of part 4 joins `BOSS_ART` (`src/ui/bosses/bake.js`).
- It appears in the lobby after world 19 the way every world does; no screen is added or changed.
- Five levels, `w20-1` to `w20-5`, in a new `src/core/levels-6.js`: four wave levels that follow the data rules `test/core/levels.test.js` enforces, and a boss level.
- Its own row in `difficulty.js` (it never falls back to the main curve, which would leave the scale), every value from 0 to 1 and no easier than world 19's.
- Pars and ratings are regenerated by their scripts (`scripts/pars.mjs`, `scripts/ratings.mjs`), never typed.
- The suite's boss tests fight every entry of `BOSSES` on its level (`test/core/boss-kit.js`). Tuning the `editor` definition of part 2 so that they pass is in scope; its acceptance tests keep holding.
- Every tuning value is a named, exported constant, defined once.

### The per-world rows

- Decision (the grill and the person, 2026-11-18): the per-world rows the suite requires are in scope: the lobby tagline and poster time (`src/ui/screens/posters.js`), the trailer voice (`src/ui/trailer/cards.js`) and the trailer typography row (`TYPE` in `src/ui/trailer/draw.js`), and so is any other per-world row a test or the renderer requires for every world. One such row is known: the film grade colour (`TINTS` in `src/fx/film.js`), which `test/fx/film.test.js:19-22` requires to differ for every world. The person said "use your imagination": the words and the typography are the builder's, in the voice of the other worlds.
- Design reference: world 19's rows in those same files, and its card in the lobby as it is in the game.

### The documents

- The design spec (`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-17-invaders-design.md`) gets the rule as R72 and world 20's row in its Worlds table.
- `README.md`, `llms.txt` and `CLAUDE.md` say 20 worlds and 100 levels.

## Registrations

Existing assertions that move, and only as far as said:

- `test/core/kinds.test.js:19-20`: the pinned lists of `TWISTS` and `BOSSES` gain `echo` and `editor` as their twentieth names.
- `test/core/levels.test.js:22-26`: the title, the pinned ids and the pinned names gain world 20; `:33-35`: 95 levels become 100; `:67-69`: 19 boss levels become 20; `:111`: `levelById('w20-1')` is no longer unknown, so the unknown id it asks for becomes one of a world that does not exist.
- `test/core/boss-data.test.js:13`: the title says twenty; the comparison at `:14` holds as it is.
- `test/ui/bosses.test.js:15`: the number of boss pictures goes from 19 to 20.
- `test/ui/lobby-keys.test.js`: the last lobby row has four cards instead of three, so the walks that end in it move by one card: the comment at `:6`, the wrap at `:28-34`, the column clamp at `:112`, and the goal-column walk at `:119-137`. Each keeps its rule (the short row wraps on itself, a column past its end clamps to the last card, up undoes down); only the ids it lands on change.
- Regenerated data, not assertions: `src/core/pars.js` and `src/core/ratings.js` gain world 20 by their scripts (`test/core/pars.test.js` and `test/core/ratings.test.js:10` compare them with `LEVELS` and `WORLDS` and hold as they are).

Existing rules that hold for the larger tables without moving: `TAGLINES` (`test/ui/posters.test.js:13-14`) and `VOICES` (`test/ui/trailer-cards.test.js:39`) have one entry a world; a poster is baked for every world from its boss level (`posters.test.js:22-24`, `posters-prefer.test.js`); every twist in `TWISTS` runs in a plain level (`test/core/twists.test.js`, `bonk.test.js`, `breath.test.js`); every boss in `BOSSES` is fought (`boss.test.js`, `boss-ai.test.js`, `boss-bots.test.js`, `predict.test.js`); every definition has art and warms (`test/ui/bosses.test.js:16`, `:110`, `warm.test.js:26`); the lobby tests that read `WORLDS.length` and `LEVELS.length`.

The pinned fingerprints of worlds 1-19 (`test/core/worlds-unchanged.test.js`, `worlds-idle.test.js`) do not move. If any other existing assertion fails, the builder stops and reports it; it is not changed.

## Acceptance tests

`node:test`, in `test/`, reading the constants and never repeating their values:

1. `WORLDS` has 20 entries; the last is id 20, "Midnight Press", twist `echo`, boss `editor`, free. There are 100 levels, and the level data rules hold for world 20.
2. World 20 has its own row in `difficulty.js`; every value is from 0 to 1 and no easier than world 19's, level for level.
3. The boss `editor` is fought to its end on `w20-5` and never charges.
4. The fingerprints of worlds 1-19 are unchanged.
5. World 20 has its lobby card after world 19 with its tagline and poster time, its trailer voice and its `TYPE` row, its own film tint, and every other per-world row a test requires of every world.
6. The whole suite is green (`npm test`) with only the assertions named above moved, and every code file is under 200 lines.

## Out of scope

- Any change to the trailer, the screens, the lobby's layout or the storage keys, beyond world 20's own per-world rows (see the decision above).
- Any change to how worlds 1-19 play.
- Any new drawing or sound (parts 3-5).
- `og.png` and `og.html` (decision: `og.png` stays out), deployment, the Pi menu.
- A new kind of power-up.
